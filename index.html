FAQ

Questions people ask.

Why does it need Accessibility and Microphone?

Microphone, to hear you while you hold the key. Accessibility, to notice the key anywhere on the Mac and to type the text into the app you're using. It never reads your screen.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the one-time model download on first launch, dictation needs no connection at all.

Which languages?

English, all accents. The model is English-only by design; that's what makes it this accurate and this fast.

How is it different from Wispr Flow?

Wispr Flow transcribes in the cloud (its own docs say transcription always occurs on the cloud) and is a subscription, $12 to $15 a month. TypeVoice transcribes on your Mac's Neural Engine, so your voice never leaves the machine, it works offline, and you buy it once. Wispr Flow covers 100+ languages; TypeVoice does English only, and does it very well.

What happens after the trial?

Dictation pauses until you buy it, once. A license key arrives by email; paste it into the License tab (or click the link on the thank-you page) and you're back. Everything you dictated stays in Summary.

Is it on the Mac App Store?

No, on purpose. Sold directly, TypeVoice can type straight into the field you're in through Accessibility instead of pasting, and updates ship the moment they're ready. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, like any other Mac app you download.

Is the price the same everywhere?

No. Dodo Payments adjusts the price to your country's purchasing power, so in many countries the checkout shows a lower local price in your own currency, taxes included. Same app, same key, same two Macs.

Can I use one license on more than one Mac?

Yes: a personal key works on two Macs at once, typically a desk Mac and a laptop. Moving to a new Mac? Press Deactivate this Mac on the old one and the seat is free. For a team, the team key covers 5 people with two Macs each.

How long can one dictation be?

As long as you like up to ten minutes; at that point the words so far are typed and you carry on with the next one. Pauses are fine, however long: think, then keep talking.

Which Macs?

Any Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.
