Which one

Both are good. Pick by temperament.

VoiceInk is the better pick if

  • You want the lowest price
  • You like choosing models, or bringing your own cloud keys
  • You want different modes per app or website
  • You dictate in languages other than English, or want it on iPhone

TypeVoice is the better pick if

  • You want it to just work, with no models or keys to set up
  • You want good cleanup without a big local LLM or a cloud key
  • You'd like to send a dictation as a voice note, or sign it
  • You're on macOS 26 and speak English

Questions

Questions people ask.

Are TypeVoice and VoiceInk both open source?

Yes, both under the GPL v3. With each, what you pay for is the signed build, updates and support.

Which is cheaper?

VoiceInk: $25 for one Mac or $39 for two. TypeVoice is $79 for two Macs. Both are one-time purchases.

Why would I pick TypeVoice over VoiceInk?

If you'd rather not choose models or set up AI enhancement. TypeVoice ships one tuned English model and a cleanup step that runs on your Mac with nothing extra to download, plus voice notes and an optional sign-off line.

Does VoiceInk send my voice anywhere?

Not with local models. Its cloud transcription and cloud AI enhancement are optional and use your own API keys. TypeVoice has no cloud option at all.
