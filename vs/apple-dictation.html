Questions

Questions people ask.

Is Apple Dictation on the Mac private?

On Apple silicon, for languages with on-device support, it runs on your Mac. On Intel Macs or other languages, Apple's guide says your speech is sent to Apple. TypeVoice always runs on your Mac and never sends audio or text anywhere.

Why pay for dictation when the Mac has it built in?

If you dictate a sentence now and then, the built-in one is fine. TypeVoice is for daily use: it removes fillers and false starts, learns your names through the Dictionary, doesn't stop when you pause, and lets you hold a key to talk.

Does Apple Dictation remove 'um' and 'uh'?

Apple's guide documents auto-punctuation and spoken commands, not filler removal or rewriting. TypeVoice removes fillers, stutters and restarts on your Mac.

Can I keep using Apple Dictation too?

Yes. TypeVoice uses its own shortcut, so the built-in Dictation key keeps working.
