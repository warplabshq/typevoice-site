Which one

Honestly, it depends.

Superwhisper is the better pick if

  • You dictate in a language other than English
  • You need it on Windows, iPhone or Android too
  • You want custom AI modes and your choice of cloud models
  • You also record meetings or transcribe files
  • You're on an Intel Mac or an older macOS

TypeVoice is the better pick if

  • You want dictation that just works, with nothing to configure
  • You want the cleanup to happen on your Mac, too
  • You'd rather pay $79 once than $85 every year
  • You want to read the code that's listening to you

Questions

Questions people ask.

Is Superwhisper local or cloud?

Both. Its free tier uses local Whisper models, and Pro adds local Parakeet and Cohere models. Pro also offers cloud speech models and, for AI modes, mostly cloud language models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini. TypeVoice has one path: everything on your Mac.

Which is cheaper, TypeVoice or Superwhisper?

TypeVoice is $79 once. Superwhisper Pro is $84.99 a year or $249.99 for a lifetime licence, and Superwhisper also has a free tier with local Whisper dictation.

Does TypeVoice have AI modes like Superwhisper?

No. TypeVoice has one well-tuned cleanup step that removes fillers, stutters and false starts and formats numbers and lists, on your Mac. It doesn't rewrite your tone or translate.

Can I try both side by side?

Yes. Give TypeVoice a different shortcut and use both for the 7-day trial. No card or account needed.
