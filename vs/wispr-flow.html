Which one

Honestly, it depends.

Wispr Flow is the better pick if

  • You dictate in a language other than English
  • You need it on Windows, iPhone or Android too
  • You want heavier AI rewriting and don't mind it happening on someone else's computer

TypeVoice is the better pick if

  • You write things that shouldn't sit on a server: client work, medical or legal notes, code under NDA, your own life
  • You'd rather pay once than every month
  • You want to be able to read the code that's listening to you
  • You're on a Mac and you speak English
