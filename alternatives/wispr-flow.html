Questions

Questions people ask.

What is the best offline alternative to Wispr Flow on Mac?

For everyday dictation that never leaves your Mac and is paid once, TypeVoice. For the lowest price with lots of options, VoiceInk. For free, Handy or FluidVoice. For transcribing recordings, MacWhisper.

Is there a free Wispr Flow alternative?

Yes: Apple's built-in Dictation, Handy and FluidVoice are free. Superwhisper and MacWhisper have free tiers. TypeVoice is free for 7 days, then $79 once.

Is there a Wispr Flow alternative without a subscription?

TypeVoice ($79), MacWhisper Pro (€64) and VoiceInk ($25–49) are one-time purchases. Superwhisper sells a $249.99 lifetime licence besides its subscription.

Does Wispr Flow work offline?

No. Wispr Flow's own docs say transcription always occurs in the cloud, so it needs an internet connection.
