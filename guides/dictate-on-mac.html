How to dictate on a Mac.

Your Mac can type what you say in any app. Here's how to turn on the built-in Dictation in under a minute, the voice commands worth knowing, and what to use when you dictate every day.

Updated October 2026 · macOS 26

Built-in Dictation

Turn it on in three steps.

1

Open Keyboard settings

Apple menu › System Settings › Keyboard, then scroll to Dictation.

2

Switch Dictation on

Pick your language and a shortcut. Recent keyboards have a Microphone key 🎤 for it.

3

Talk

Click into any text field, press the shortcut, speak, and press it again to stop.

Questions

Questions people ask.

How do I turn on dictation on a Mac?

Open System Settings › Keyboard, scroll to Dictation and switch it on. Choose a language and a shortcut, then press the shortcut in any text field to start talking.

What is the dictation shortcut on Mac?

Recent Mac keyboards have a Microphone key (🎤) that starts Dictation. You can pick a different shortcut in System Settings › Keyboard › Dictation › Shortcut.

Does Mac dictation work offline?

On Apple silicon Macs, for languages with on-device support, yes. On Intel Macs or other languages, Apple's guide says speech is sent to Apple. TypeVoice always runs offline.

Why does Mac dictation stop by itself?

Apple's Dictation stops after about 30 seconds of silence. TypeVoice keeps listening while you hold the key, however long you pause.

How do I add punctuation when dictating on a Mac?

Say it: "comma", "period", "question mark", "new line" or "new paragraph". Dictation also adds punctuation automatically in supported languages.
