Support

We answer within a day or two. Email support.

It asks for Microphone and Accessibility. Why?

Microphone, to hear you while you hold the key. Accessibility, to notice the key you hold anywhere on the Mac and to type the finished text into the app you're using. TypeVoice never reads what is on your screen. Both switches live in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

The 🌐 key opens the emoji picker.

System Settings › Keyboard › "Press 🌐 key to" → Do Nothing. Or pick another trigger under Settings › Dictating › Custom and press the key or combination you want: Right ⌘, Right ⌥, ⌥Space, anything.

Accessibility looks on but nothing happens.

Flip the TypeVoice switch off and on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, then quit and reopen the app. The menu bar shows a fix-it item whenever the permission is missing.

Nothing was typed, but the text is in Summary.

Some places can't take text (the desktop, a button, a list). The pill offers a Copy button in that case, and every dictation is kept in Summary where you can copy it later.

"TypeVoice can't be opened" / "Apple could not verify…"

The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so a normal download opens with no warning. If macOS still objects, the download was probably interrupted: delete it and download again from this site. Never disable Gatekeeper for it.

Where is my data?

Privacy › Reveal in Finder opens the folder. Summary is an SQLite database, the dictionary is JSON, and recordings (if you turned on Offer the audio after each dictation) are small AAC files. Summary › Export writes everything as text, Markdown, CSV or JSON. Delete Everything removes all of it.

How do I send the audio instead of the text?

Settings › Indicator › Offer the audio after each dictation. From then on a Drag audio chip appears on the pill after you speak, and every row in Summary can be played or dragged. Drop it into iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack or an email. Recordings are AAC, about 240 KB a minute, stay on your Mac, and are deleted after a week unless you choose a longer retention (or forever) next to that setting.

Can I start dictation from Shortcuts, Raycast or a Stream Deck?

Yes. Open the URL typevoice://toggle to start or stop a hands-free session; typevoice://start, typevoice://stop and typevoice://cancel do one thing each. In Shortcuts use the Open URLs action; in a terminal, open typevoice://toggle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the one-time model download on first launch, dictation needs no connection at all. A licensed app re-checks its key once a week when it can; a Mac that stays offline keeps working for 30 days before it needs to.

Which languages?

English, all accents. The recognition model is English-only by design; it is what makes it this accurate and this fast.

Buying, license keys and other Macs

Buy once from the pricing section; Dodo Payments handles the checkout and emails you a license key straight away (the thank-you page can also hand it to the app directly). Paste the key into TypeVoice › License. One key works on up to 3 Macs; to move to a new Mac, press Deactivate this Mac on the old one first, or email us and we'll free the seat.

Lost your key?

Search your email for "Dodo Payments". If it's gone, write to support from the address you bought with and we'll resend it.

Refunds

Within 14 days of purchase, email support and we refund in full, no questions asked. Dodo Payments returns the money to the original payment method, usually within a few business days.

Updates

The app checks for updates once a day and offers them in place; Settings › General has Check for Updates and a switch for the automatic check. Updates are signed twice: by Apple's notarization and by our own update key.

Which Macs?

macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. The speech model runs on the Neural Engine, which is why it is this fast and this light on the battery.

Keyboard cheatsheet

Hold the trigger to dictate; release to type. Tap it twice to keep listening hands-free, then tap once to stop. Or switch to tap-to-toggle in Settings › Dictating. esc cancels. With voice commands on, say "new line", "new paragraph", "bullet …" or "number one …, number two …" to shape the text; a pause after a sentence starts a new paragraph. The same card lives in the app under Help › What You Can Say.
