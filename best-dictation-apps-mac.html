How to choose

Three questions decide it.

1

Where may your voice go?

If what you say shouldn't reach a server, pick an app that runs on your Mac: TypeVoice, VoiceInk, Handy, FluidVoice, or MacWhisper and Superwhisper with local models.

2

Which languages?

English only: any of them. Many languages: Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, MacWhisper, or Apple's built-in Dictation.

3

Rent or own?

Wispr Flow and Superwhisper Pro are subscriptions. TypeVoice, MacWhisper and VoiceInk are paid once. Apple Dictation, Handy and FluidVoice are free.

Questions

Questions people ask.

What is the best free dictation app for Mac?

Apple's built-in Dictation is free and good for occasional use. Handy (MIT licensed) and FluidVoice are free, open-source apps that run on your Mac. Superwhisper, MacWhisper and Wispr Flow also have free tiers with limits.

Which Mac dictation apps work offline?

TypeVoice, VoiceInk, Handy and FluidVoice run on your Mac. MacWhisper and Superwhisper work offline with their local models. Apple Dictation works offline for on-device languages on Apple silicon. Wispr Flow needs a connection.

Which Mac dictation apps are open source?

TypeVoice and VoiceInk (GPL v3), Handy (MIT) and FluidVoice (GPL v3, with a closed AI runtime). Wispr Flow, Superwhisper and MacWhisper are closed.

Which is best for many languages?

Wispr Flow and Superwhisper list 100+ languages, MacWhisper 100, and Apple Dictation dozens. TypeVoice is English only.

Is this list biased?

We make TypeVoice, so yes, we have a stake. We've kept to facts from each maker's own pages and said plainly where other apps are the better pick.
