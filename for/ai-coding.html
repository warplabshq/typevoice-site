Questions

Questions people ask.

Does TypeVoice work with Claude Code in the terminal?

Yes. It types into Terminal, iTerm, Ghostty, Warp and other terminals the same way your keyboard does, so it works with Claude Code, Codex, Aider or any other command-line tool.

Do my prompts leave my Mac?

TypeVoice itself sends nothing: speech becomes text on your Mac's Neural Engine. The text then goes wherever you typed it, so your AI app sends your prompt to its provider as it always does. TypeVoice adds no extra service in between.

Can it handle code words like kubectl or camelCase names?

A built-in developer word pack covers common tools, and your Dictionary handles your own names and terms. It's dictation for prompts and prose; it doesn't write code syntax for you.

What is the sign-off note for?

When an AI coding assistant sees an unusual spelling, it may assume it's a real identifier and go looking for it. A short line like "(dictated; spellings may be off)" tells it the text was spoken. You choose the wording in Settings › Style.

How much does it cost?

Free for 7 days, then $79 once for two Macs, with updates included. There's no subscription.
